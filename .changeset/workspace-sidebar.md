The project rail is gone: every workspace now sits in one flat sidebar list. Multi-repo workspaces show one chip per repo with its PR and checks, and the Repository filter matches a workspace on any of its repos. Add repo, Import sessions and Manage repos moved into the sidebar's filter menu, a new Home row opens Home without closing any chats, and blocked workspaces show an amber needs-you count on their row. Your saved sidebar filters carry over automatically.
